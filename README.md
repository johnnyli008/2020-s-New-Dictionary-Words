A compiled reference of words and phrases reported as newly added
to major English dictionaries between 2020 and 2026.

Compiled: 11 October 2026

SOURCES CONSULTED
- Merriam-Webster (merriam-webster.com; new-word batches and the 12th
  edition of Merriam-Webster's Collegiate Dictionary)
- Oxford English Dictionary / Oxford Languages (quarterly OED updates)
- Dictionary.com (word drops and updates)
- Collins Dictionary
- Press coverage carrying the official lists: Associated Press (AP),
  NPR, BBC, ABC News / GMA, CBC, Mental Floss, USA Today, The Guardian,
  Washington Post, Time, Business Insider, Chico Enterprise-Record,
  WPMI/NBC15, infoDOCKET, New Statesman, OUP Blog, and others.

METHOD NOTES
- Each entry lists the word, part of speech, a short definition, and the
  dictionary (via the news source that published its official list).
- Batch sizes shown in entry sources (e.g. "Sept 2023 update (690 words)")
  are the totals announced by the dictionary; this document lists the
  notable/confirmed entries from each batch, not every headword.
- Merriam-Webster.com and Dictionary.com block automated access, so their
  lists were reconstructed from publishers who printed the official lists.
- Where a dictionary's quarterly update is dated at year-end but announced
  the following year (OED practice), the note appears in the source tag.
- "Word of the Year" picks are not new words per se; where included they
  are marked as such.
